Welcome!

Welcome to the Physical Gaming Collection, this site is a passion project based upon my life-long love for video games, and a tad bit of longing for older times. If you've taken an interest into this page, you've likely noticed a shift in the world of gaming. Games can still be purchased in physical drive or disc forms, but for the most part companies and consumers alike have moved on to digital downloads and digital codes to aquire new titles. This website is dedicated to games that never had fully digital options, or games where digital downloads simply just weren't as popular!

What do I do here?

Feel free to look around, each page is full of information to take a look at! This is the Home page, the news section is updated frequently and will provide you with information on completed or updated events through this page! In About, there is more information on the purpose of this site and a bit of information on how the gaming market has gotten to a point where the goal is to be fully digital. In "My Top 10 Games," there will be a list of my favorite games that were either sold completely or mostly through physical cartridges or discs! The Gallery will hold images related to early development, old marketing campaigns, character designing, and so much more that went into retro/older games! Finally, the contact page allows you to get in touch with the creator of this site. We can help you find stores near you dedicated to selling retro and physical gaming media, reputable online sellers that can ship retro games directly to your house, and much more!
